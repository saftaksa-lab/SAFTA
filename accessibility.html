Accessibility

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Our commitment

The Alliance is committed to making this website usable by the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We target conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

What we have implemented

  • Semantic HTML structure with a single main landmark and a logical heading order.
  • Keyboard access to all interactive controls, with a visible focus state.
  • Text contrast that meets AA thresholds against its background.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Full support for prefers-reduced-motion — all animation is disabled when the setting is on.
  • Responsive layout that reflows without horizontal scrolling down to 320px.

Known limitations

  • The Arabic (RTL) version of the site is not yet published.
  • Some third-party embedded content may not fully meet AA.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, email secretariat@safta.sa with the page address and a short description. We aim to respond within three working days.
