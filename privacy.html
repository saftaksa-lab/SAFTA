Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains what personal data the Alliance collects through this website, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

2. Data we collect

  • Data you provide: name, entity, entity type, work email, phone, website, area of interest and any message you send through the registration, membership, idea-submission or contact forms.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and pages viewed.
  • Cookies: see the Cookie Notice.

3. Why we use it

  • To review and respond to registrations, membership requests and inquiries.
  • To route submitted ideas to the relevant working group.
  • To send operational updates about the Alliance where you have asked to receive them.
  • To measure site performance and improve content.

4. Legal basis

Personal data is processed on the basis of your consent, or where processing is necessary to respond to a request you have made, in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

5. Sharing

Data is shared internally within the Alliance and, where relevant to your request, with the member entity concerned. We do not sell personal data. Service providers who process data on our behalf are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Retention

Registration and inquiry records are retained for as long as needed to handle the request and to meet record-keeping obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Requests can be sent to secretariat@safta.sa.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
